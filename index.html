ABOUT ROAD SAFETY

Road safety means protecting drivers, passengers, cyclists and pedestrians. Following traffic rules and driving carefully can save thousands of lives.

IMPORTANT ROAD RULES

01. Wear Seatbelts

Always wear your seatbelt while travelling.

02. Follow Speed Limits

Drive within the permitted speed limit.

03. No Mobile Phones

Never use your phone while driving.

04. Wear a Helmet

Always wear a helmet when riding a motorcycle.
